A Shahdara Family Kitchen Built on Honest Flavors

Zaiqa & Grill started as a welcoming family eatery in Shahdara, Lahore in 2016 with one simple goal: serve clean, freshly cooked desi meals that feel like a true gathering at home.

Conveniently located along the GT Road corridor near Begum Kot and Ravi Bridge, we never pre-cook our gravies. Every karahi, seekh kabab, and tandoori naan is prepared hot to order for your table.
